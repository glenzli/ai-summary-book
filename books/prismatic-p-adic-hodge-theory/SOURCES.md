# 资料源

本文档记录《Prismatic / p-adic Hodge Theory》教材的主要资料源。正文不得复制原文；所有内容均应重写并标明依赖边界。

初次核查日期：2026-07-08。核心 PDF 定理号复核：2026-07-11。

## Classical $p$-adic Hodge theory

- Jean-Marc Fontaine, period rings and admissible representations. 用途：$B_{\mathrm{HT}}$、$B_{\mathrm{dR}}$、$B_{\mathrm{cris}}$、$B_{\mathrm{st}}$、$B$-admissibility 和 filtered $\varphi$-module 口径。
- Gerd Faltings, $p$-adic Hodge theory and almost purity. 用途：classical comparison theorem 和 almost purity 背景；在正文中作为外部输入。
- Takeshi Tsuji, $p$-adic etale cohomology and crystalline cohomology in the semistable reduction case. 用途：semistable comparison theorem 外部输入。
- Olivier Brinon and Brian Conrad, *CMI Summer School Notes on p-adic Hodge Theory*. 用途：classical period rings、$B$-admissibility 和 comparison theorem 的教材型参考。
- Laurent Berger, notes and papers on $p$-adic representations. 用途：$(\varphi,\Gamma)$-modules、de Rham/crystalline/semistable representations 的结构背景。
- Kiran Kedlaya and Ruochuan Liu, relative $p$-adic Hodge theory. 用途：relative period sheaves and relative comparison 的背景，作为后续扩写来源。

## Perfectoid spaces and pro-etale foundations

- Peter Scholze, “Perfectoid Spaces,” *Publ. Math. IHES*, 2012. 用途：perfectoid spaces、tilting 和 almost purity 背景。
- Peter Scholze, “$p$-adic Hodge theory for rigid-analytic varieties,” *Forum of Mathematics, Pi*, 2013. 用途：pro-etale period sheaves 和 rigid-analytic comparison 外部输入。
- Bhargav Bhatt and Peter Scholze, “The pro-étale topology for schemes,” *Astérisque* 369, 2015. 用途：pro-etale site and completed etale cohomology background。

## Integral and prismatic theory

- Bhargav Bhatt, Matthew Morrow, Peter Scholze, “Integral $p$-adic Hodge theory,” arXiv:1602.03148, v3 2019-01-15, final version. 用途：Theorems 1.8, 14.3 的 $A_{\inf}$ 四个 derived comparisons；Definition 1.5 的 BKF modules；Theorems 4.28, 14.5 (iii) 的 finite-free classification 与 cohomological torsion thresholds；Corollary 6.5, Propositions 6.7, 6.12, Lemmas 6.19--6.20 的 $L\eta$ package。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03148>。
- Bhargav Bhatt, Matthew Morrow, Peter Scholze, “Topological Hochschild homology and integral $p$-adic Hodge theory,” arXiv:1802.03261 v2 final 2019-04-09; *Publ. Math. IHES* 129, 2019. 用途：Theorem 1.2 的 Breuil--Kisin cohomology；Theorem 11.2 的 local construction；Theorem 1.12 (5), Theorem 1.15 与 Theorem 10.1 的 syntomic fibre/products 和 nearby cycles。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03261>。
- Bhargav Bhatt and Peter Scholze, “Prisms and Prismatic Cohomology,” arXiv:1905.08229, v4 2022-01-12. 用途：§1.2 的 derived completion；Definition 3.2, Proposition 3.5, Lemma 3.7 与 Corollary 3.12/Definition 4.1 的 prism/site；Theorems 4.11, 5.2, 6.3, 9.1 与 Corollaries 4.12, 15.4, 15.5 的基础 comparisons；Theorem 17.2 的 $A_{\inf}$ identification。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08229>。
- Bhargav Bhatt and Peter Scholze, “Prismatic $F$-crystals and crystalline Galois representations,” arXiv:2106.14735, v2 2023-09-12. 用途：Definition 4.1 的 localized Frobenius/effective convention；Theorem 5.6 的 crystalline lattice equivalence；Examples 2.6, 4.3 与 Theorem 7.9 的 Breuil--Kisin evaluation 和 Kisin full faithfulness。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.14735>。
- Bhargav Bhatt and Jacob Lurie, “The prismatization of $p$-adic formal schemes,” arXiv:2201.06124, v1 2022-01-16. 用途：Cartier-Witt stack、prismatic crystals 的 quasicoherent sheaf 解释；作为前沿接口使用。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06124>。
- Dmitry Kubrak and Artem Prikhodko, “$p$-adic Hodge theory for Artin stacks,” arXiv:2105.05319, 2021-05-11. 用途：Artin stacks 中 integral $p$-adic Hodge theory、prismatic cohomology and Breuil-Kisin modules 的应用边界。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.05319>。

## Nygaard, syntomic, motivic and coefficient directions

- Bhatt-Morrow-Scholze, syntomic complexes and $p$-adic Tate twists in integral $p$-adic Hodge theory. 用途：syntomic cohomology and Tate twists background。
- Matthew Morrow, overview notes on $A_{\inf}$-cohomology. 用途：BMS construction 解释性辅助，不作为唯一核心来源。
- Takeshi Tsuji, “Prismatic cohomology and $A_{\inf}$-cohomology with coefficients,” arXiv:2509.04954, 2025-09-05. 用途：带系数 prismatic cohomology 与 $A_{\inf}$-cohomology 的近期研究边界；不得直接作为基础定理链。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.04954>。

## 2025-2026 研究边界

以下资料只作为研究边界入口。除非后续章节完成独立验证，不得把其中新结果写成正文定理。

- Shachar Carmeli and Tony Feng, “Prismatic Steenrod operations and arithmetic duality on Brauer groups,” arXiv:2507.13471, 2025-07-17. 用途：spectral syntomic cohomology、syntomic Steenrod algebra、spectral prismatic $F$-gauges 的前沿入口。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.13471>。
- Takeshi Tsuji, “Prismatic cohomology and $A_{\inf}$-cohomology with coefficients,” arXiv:2509.04954, 2025-09-05. 用途：coefficients and $q$-Higgs module interface。
- Xiaoyu Qu and Jiahong Yu, “Rational Hodge-Tate prismatic crystals of quasi-l.c.i algebras and non-abelian $p$-adic Hodge theory,” arXiv:2511.03458, 2025-11-05. 用途：non-abelian $p$-adic Hodge theory 研究边界。
- Emiliano Ambrosi, Rachel Newton, Margherita Pagano, “Wild Brauer classes via prismatic cohomology,” arXiv:2509.22025, 2025-09-26. 用途：prismatic cohomology 在 Brauer classes 与 weak approximation 问题中的应用边界。
- Shubhodip Mondal and Martin Olsson, “Height 1 Group Schemes and Prismatic F-Gauges,” arXiv:2604.16066, v1 2026-04-17. 用途：prismatic $F$-gauges 与 finite flat group schemes 的最新边界。链接：<https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16066>。
- Kazuhiro Ito, “Deformation theory for prismatic $G$-displays,” arXiv:2306.05361, 2023-06-08. 用途：prismatic $G$-displays、local Shimura varieties 和 $F$-gauges 接口。
- Naoki Imai, Hiroki Kato, Alex Youcis, “The prismatic realization functor for Shimura varieties of abelian type,” arXiv:2310.08472, 2023-10-12. 用途：Shimura varieties 的 prismatic realization 与 integral $p$-adic Hodge theory 前沿接口。
