# 资料源与外部输入清单

核查日期：2026-07-08。以下来源用于定位定义、核心定理、外部输入和研究边界；正文不会复制来源原文。

## 核心历史与 HMS 断言

- Maxim Kontsevich, *Homological Algebra of Mirror Symmetry*, ICM 1994, arXiv:alg-geom/9411018.  
  用途：HMS 原始同调代数定式、历史入口、镜像等价的范畴化口径。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9411018

## dg 与 $A_\infty$ 基础

- Bernhard Keller, *On differential graded categories*, arXiv:math/0601185.  
  用途：dg category、enhancement、derived Morita theory 的基础资料源。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0601185
- Kenji Lefevre-Hasegawa, *Sur les A-infini catégories*, arXiv:math/0310337.  
  用途：$A_\infty$ categories、modules、Yoneda、pretriangulated structures 和 quasi-equivalence。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0310337
- Vladimir Drinfeld, *DG quotients of DG categories*, arXiv:math/0210114.  
  用途：dg quotient、Verdier quotient 的增强模型。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0210114

## B-side 与 Fourier-Mukai 技术

- Daniel Huybrechts, *Fourier-Mukai Transforms in Algebraic Geometry*, Oxford University Press, 2006.  
  用途：$\mathrm D^b\operatorname{Coh}$、Fourier-Mukai kernels、导出等价的标准资料源。
- Bondal-Orlov reconstruction 与 Orlov representability 相关文献。  
  用途：导出范畴等价、Fourier-Mukai 型函子的外部输入；后续 theorem locator 需逐条定位。

## A-side 与 Fukaya categories

- Paul Seidel, *Fukaya Categories and Picard-Lefschetz Theory*, European Mathematical Society, 2008.  
  用途：exact Fukaya categories、Fukaya-Seidel categories、Picard-Lefschetz 技术和可计算例子。
- Kenji Fukaya, Yong-Geun Oh, Hiroshi Ohta, Kaoru Ono, *Lagrangian Intersection Floer Theory: Anomaly and Obstruction*, AMS/IP.  
  用途：一般 Lagrangian Floer theory、obstruction、bounding cochains、Kuranishi/virtual 技术的外部输入。
- Denis Auroux, *A beginner's introduction to Fukaya categories*, arXiv:1301.7056.  
  用途：教学性路线、Fukaya categories 与应用概览；不作为技术定理的最终来源。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.7056
- Alexander Polishchuk, Eric Zaslow, *Categorical Mirror Symmetry: The Elliptic Curve*, arXiv:math/9801119.  
  用途：椭圆曲线 HMS 的标准源。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9801119
- Mohammed Abouzaid, *Morse Homology, Tropical Geometry, and Homological Mirror Symmetry for Toric Varieties*, arXiv:math/0610004.  
  用途：toric varieties 的 HMS、tropical/Morse 模型。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0610004
- David Nadler, Eric Zaslow, *Constructible Sheaves and the Fukaya Category*, arXiv:math/0604379.  
  用途：cotangent Fukaya category 与 constructible sheaves。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0604379
- Nicholas Sheridan, *Homological Mirror Symmetry for Calabi-Yau hypersurfaces in projective space*, arXiv:1111.0632.  
  用途：projective-space Calabi-Yau hypersurfaces 的 HMS 证明型结果。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.0632

## Wrapped、sectorial descent 与 microlocal 模型

- Sheel Ganatra, John Pardon, Vivek Shende, *Covariantly functorial wrapped Floer theory on Liouville sectors*, arXiv:1706.03152.  
  用途：Liouville sectors、wrapped Fukaya category、协变函子性。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03152
- Sheel Ganatra, John Pardon, Vivek Shende, *Sectorial descent for wrapped Fukaya categories*, arXiv:1809.03427.  
  用途：sectorial descent、stop removal、generation、Kunneth formula 等外部输入。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.03427
- Sheel Ganatra, John Pardon, Vivek Shende, *Microlocal Morse theory of wrapped Fukaya categories*, arXiv:1809.08807.  
  用途：wrapped Fukaya categories 与 constructible sheaves/microsupport 的等价。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08807
- Zachary Sylvan, *Orlov and Viterbo functors in partially wrapped Fukaya categories*, arXiv:1908.02317.  
  用途：partially wrapped categories 中 functoriality、spherical functors、Viterbo transfer 的研究入口。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.02317

## 已核查的近期 HMS 例子与边界

- Mohammed Abouzaid, Denis Auroux, *Homological mirror symmetry for hypersurfaces in $(\mathbb C^\ast)^n$*, arXiv:2111.06543.  
  用途：hypersurfaces in algebraic tori、fiberwise wrapped Fukaya category、quasi-embedding 类型结果。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06543
- Yanki Lekili, Kazushi Ueda, *Homological mirror symmetry for Rabinowitz Fukaya categories of Milnor fibers of Brieskorn-Pham singularities*, arXiv:2406.15915.  
  用途：Rabinowitz Fukaya categories、Milnor fibers、matrix factorizations 的近期例子。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.15915
- Sheel Ganatra, Andrew Hanlon, Jeff Hicks, Daniel Pomerleano, Nick Sheridan, *Homological mirror symmetry for Batyrev mirror pairs*, arXiv:2406.05272.  
  用途：Batyrev mirror pairs 的 2024 证明型研究边界。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05272
- Veronica Pasquarella, *Advancements in Functorial Homological Mirror Symmetry*, arXiv:2502.06951.  
  用途：functorial HMS 的 2025 研究边界入口；暂不作为基础正文定理。链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06951
