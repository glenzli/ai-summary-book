# 资料源

本文档记录《范畴论》教材的主要资料源。正文不得复制原文；所有内容均应重写并标明依赖边界。

## 普通范畴论

- Saunders Mac Lane, *Categories for the Working Mathematician*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1998.
- Francis Borceux, *Handbook of Categorical Algebra*, Vols. 1-3, Cambridge University Press, 1994.
- Steve Awodey, *Category Theory*, 2nd ed., Oxford University Press, 2010.
- Tom Leinster, *Basic Category Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 2014; arXiv:1612.09375.
- Emily Riehl, *Category Theory in Context*, Dover, 2016. Author PDF: <https://emilyriehl.github.io/files/context.pdf>.
- S. Mac Lane and I. Moerdijk, *Sheaves in Geometry and Logic*, Springer, 1992.

## 富范畴、幺半范畴与可表现范畴

- G. M. Kelly, *Basic Concepts of Enriched Category Theory*, Cambridge University Press, 1982; Reprints in Theory and Applications of Categories, No. 10.
- Brian Day, *On Closed Categories of Functors*, Reports of the Midwest Category Seminar IV, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 137, Springer, 1970.
- Jiří Adámek and Jiří Rosický, *Locally Presentable and Accessible Categories*, Cambridge University Press, 1994.
- Peter Johnstone, *Sketches of an Elephant: A Topos Theory Compendium*, Oxford University Press, 2002.
- Alexander Grothendieck et al., *Théorie des topos et cohomologie étale des schémas (SGA 4)*.
- N. Popescu, *Abelian Categories with Applications to Rings and Modules*, Academic Press, 1973.

## 模型范畴与同伦代数

- Daniel Quillen, *Homotopical Algebra*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 43, Springer, 1967.
- Mark Hovey, *Model Categories*, Mathematical Surveys and Monographs 63, AMS, 1999.
- Philip Hirschhorn, *Model Categories and Their Localizations*, AMS, 2003.
- Paul Goerss and John Jardine, *Simplicial Homotopy Theory*, Birkhäuser, 1999.
- W. G. Dwyer and D. M. Kan, papers on simplicial localizations and hammock localization.
- Julia Bergner, papers on the model category of simplicial categories.
- Charles Rezk, papers on complete Segal spaces and homotopy theories.
- Clark Barwick and Dan Kan, work on relative categories and models for homotopy theories.
- Jean-Marc Cordier and Timothy Porter, work on homotopy coherent category theory.

## 同伦范畴论与 $\infty$-范畴

- Jacob Lurie, *Higher Topos Theory*, Princeton University Press, 2009. Author page: <https://www.math.ias.edu/~lurie/papers/HTT.pdf>.

- Emily Riehl and Dominic Verity, papers on $\infty$-cosmoi and adjunctions between $\infty$-categories.
- André Joyal, *The Theory of Quasi-Categories and its Applications*, lecture notes.
- Denis-Charles Cisinski, *Higher Categories and Homotopical Algebra*, Cambridge University Press, 2019.
- Vladimir Hinich, *Lectures on $\infty$-Categories*, Cambridge University Press, 2022.
- Charles Rezk, notes and papers on complete Segal spaces.
- J. Peter May, *The Geometry of Iterated Loop Spaces*, Springer, 1972.
- Boardman and Vogt, *Homotopy Invariant Algebraic Structures on Topological Spaces*, Springer, 1973.
- Carlos Simpson, *Homotopy Theory of Higher Categories*, Cambridge University Press, 2012.
- Beilinson, Bernstein and Deligne, *Faisceaux pervers*, Astérisque 100, 1982.
- Jean-Louis Verdier, work on derived categories, duality, and six operations.
- Masaki Kashiwara and Pierre Schapira, *Sheaves on Manifolds*, Springer, 1990.
- Joseph Ayoub, work on the six operations in motivic homotopy theory.
- Denis-Charles Cisinski and Frédéric Déglise, work on triangulated categories of mixed motives and six operations.
- Dennis Gaitsgory and Nick Rozenblyum, *A Study in Derived Algebraic Geometry*.
- Bertrand Toën, papers on derived Morita theory and dg-categories.
- Bernhard Keller, papers and notes on dg categories, derived categories, and dg enhancements.
- Gonçalo Tabuada, work on dg categories, noncommutative motives, and Morita homotopy theory.
- Andrew Blumberg, David Gepner, and Gonçalo Tabuada, papers on universal properties of localizing invariants.
- Alberto Canonaco and Paolo Stellari, papers on uniqueness of dg enhancements.
- David Ayala and John Francis, papers on factorization homology and topological field theories.
- Michael Shulman, papers on framed bicategories, equipments, and indexed categories.
- Marco Grandis and Robert Paré, papers on double categories and equipments.
- Rune Haugseng, papers on iterated spans and higher Morita categories.
- Amnon Neeman, *Triangulated Categories*, Princeton University Press, 2001.
- Robert W. Thomason, papers on localization and classification of triangulated subcategories.
- Mark Hovey, John Palmieri and Neil Strickland, *Axiomatic Stable Homotopy Theory*, Memoirs AMS, 1997.

## 当前章节依赖

- 第一章主要依赖 Mac Lane、Leinster 和 Riehl 的普通范畴论定义。
- 第二至第七章主要依赖 Mac Lane、Borceux、Riehl 和 Leinster。
- 第八至第十一章主要依赖 Mac Lane、Kelly、Day 和 Riehl。
- 第十二至第十四章主要依赖 Adámek-Rosický、Johnstone、Mac Lane-Moerdijk、SGA 4 和 Borceux。
- 第十五至第二十三章主要依赖 Quillen、Hovey、Dwyer-Kan、Joyal、Lurie、Riehl-Verity、Kerodon、Cisinski、Hinich、BBD、Toën、Francis 和 Ayala-Francis。
- 第十七章的单纯集、horn 和 quasi-category 定义依赖 Lurie、Riehl-Verity、Kerodon 和 Joyal 讲义。
- 第二十三章的 presentable $\infty$-categories、accessible localizations 和 $\operatorname{Pr}^L$ 主要依赖 Lurie HTT、Lurie HA、Riehl-Verity 和 Kerodon。
- 第二十四章的 profunctor、Cauchy completion、weighted colimit 和 correspondence 主要依赖 Bénabou、Street、Kelly、Mac Lane、Lurie HTT/HA。
- 第二十五章的 enriched profunctor、equipment 和 Beck-Chevalley 条件主要依赖 Kelly、Street、Grandis-Paré、Shulman、Lurie 和 Haugseng。
- 第二十六章的 compact generation、Brown representability 和 Bousfield localization 主要依赖 Brown、Neeman、Thomason、Lurie HA 和 Hovey-Palmieri-Strickland。
- 第二十七章的 dg categories、dg modules、pretriangulated enhancements、derived Morita theory 和 Hochschild 型 Morita 不变量主要依赖 Keller、Toën、Tabuada、Lurie HA、Canonaco-Stellari 和 Blumberg-Gepner-Tabuada。
- 第二十八章的 six functor formalism、base change、projection formula、recollement、Verdier duality 和 purity 主要依赖 Grothendieck、Verdier、Deligne、BBD、Ayoub、Cisinski-Déglise、Kashiwara-Schapira 和 Gaitsgory-Rozenblyum。
- 第二十九章的 relative categories、simplicial localization、Dwyer-Kan equivalences、coherent nerve、complete Segal spaces 和模型比较主要依赖 Dwyer-Kan、Rezk、Bergner、Barwick-Kan、Cordier-Porter、Lurie HTT 和 Riehl-Verity。
- 附录 E 的 join、slice、marked simplicial sets、scaled simplicial sets、Joyal 模型结构和 Cartesian model structure 主要依赖 Lurie HTT、Joyal、Cisinski、Kerodon 和 Riehl-Verity。

## 引用边界

- 本书初稿中的基础定义可由上述多部教材共同核对。
- 对于大型结构定理，例如 Kan 延拓存在性、可表现范畴定理、Joyal 模型结构、straightening/unstraightening、高阶 topos 的 Giraud 型定理，应在相应章节中标为“外部输入定理”或给出完整证明路线。
