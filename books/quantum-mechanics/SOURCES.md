# 资料源

本文件记录教材核心资料源。正文不用资料原文，而用本书自己的中文表述。

## 数学基础

- John von Neumann, *Mathematical Foundations of Quantum Mechanics*.
- Michael Reed and Barry Simon, *Methods of Modern Mathematical Physics*,
  Vol. II, Theorem X.12（Kato-Rellich），以及 Vol. III, Chapter XI
  （散射理论）。
- Brian C. Hall, *Quantum Theory for Mathematicians*.
- Gerald Teschl, *Mathematical Methods in Quantum Mechanics*, 2nd ed.,
  §8.3, Theorem 8.5（谐振子与 Hermite 完备性），以及 §12.3,
  Theorems 12.11--12.12（短程势谱性质与渐近完备性）。
- Walter Rudin, *Functional Analysis*.
- Michael Taylor, *Partial Differential Equations*.

## 物理量子力学

- J. J. Sakurai and Jim Napolitano, *Modern Quantum Mechanics*.
- R. Shankar, *Principles of Quantum Mechanics*.
- L. D. Landau and E. M. Lifshitz, *Quantum Mechanics: Non-Relativistic Theory*.
- Claude Cohen-Tannoudji, Bernard Diu, Franck Laloe, *Quantum Mechanics*.
- Leslie Ballentine, *Quantum Mechanics: A Modern Development*.
- Berthold-Georg Englert, *Lectures on Quantum Mechanics*.

## 测量、开放系统与信息

- E. B. Davies, *Quantum Theory of Open Systems*.
- Heinz-Peter Breuer and Francesco Petruccione, *The Theory of Open Quantum Systems*.
- Michael A. Nielsen and Isaac L. Chuang, *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*.
- Mark M. Wilde, *Quantum Information Theory*.

## 外部输入策略

谱定理、Stone 定理、Kato-Rellich 定理、Wigner 定理、Kraus/Stinespring 表示、绝热定理、散射完备性和路径积分严格构造等结果在本书中按需作为外部输入定理。具体依赖见 [THEOREM_DEPENDENCIES.md](THEOREM_DEPENDENCIES.md) 与 [D_external_theorem_index.md](D_external_theorem_index.md)。
